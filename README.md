## Job opportunity

[Original job posting — Solar Turbines careers](https://solarturbines.jobs.cz/vacancy-detail?r=detail&id=2001237017)

A junior role in the Solar Digital AI team, developing generative AI applications for a digital platform supporting an industrial gas turbine fleet. The focus is on RAG and LLM applications, with opportunities to work on AI agents and multi-step workflows.

Responsibilities include document ingestion and retrieval pipelines, chunking, embeddings, vector search, evaluation and technical documentation. The role involves collaboration with software engineers, data scientists, mechanical and field engineers, analytics specialists and service teams.

The position calls for relevant technical education, Python, practical understanding of RAG, experience with LLMs or their APIs, software engineering fundamentals, Git, Docker and familiarity with cloud platforms. Agent frameworks, evaluation, CI/CD, MLOps and AWS/Bedrock are additional advantages.

**Location:** Prague. **Working arrangement:** five days per week in the office.
